Puneri Pangat

Puneri Pangat is a traditional Maharashtrian restaurant that brings the authentic taste of Pune to your plate.

We serve delicious homemade-style food prepared with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes.

From spicy Misal Pav to tasty Pithla Bhakri and sweet Puran Poli, every dish is made with love and a touch of Puneri style.
